The 12 Clubs Two leagues.
One obsession.

NPB splits into the Central League (CL) and the Pacific League (PL). Six clubs each, endless rivalries.

球団

CL

Central League

The historic six — セリーグ

Central League セントラル・リーグ
01

Yomiuri Giants

TokyoHome — Tokyo DomeEst. 1934

Japan's most famous club — "Kyojin" to their fans. They hold the most Japan Series titles in NPB history and play in the heart of Tokyo.

02

Hanshin Tigers

Osaka / HyogoHome — Koshien StadiumEst. 1935

The Giants' eternal rivals, 2023 champions (a first title in 38 years) and back-to-back Central League pennant winners in 2025–26 — a club first. Their home, Koshien, is a cathedral of Japanese baseball.

03

Chunichi Dragons

NagoyaHome — Vantelin Dome NagoyaEst. 1936

The pride of the Chubu region, known for strong pitching and a famously loyal local following in Japan's manufacturing heartland.

04

Yakult Swallows

TokyoHome — Meiji Jingu StadiumEst. 1950

A small-market club with a giant heart, playing next to the forest of Meiji Jingu Shrine. Famous for developing homegrown talent.

05

Hiroshima Toyo Carp

HiroshimaHome — Mazda Zoom-Zoom StadiumEst. 1949

"The people's club" — a symbol of resilience for the Chugoku region, famous for loyalty and one of NPB's most devoted fan cultures.

06

Yokohama DeNA BayStars

YokohamaHome — Yokohama StadiumEst. 1950

The stylish bay-side club and 2024 Japan Series champions — proof that underdogs can rise to the top of the Central League.

PL

Pacific League

The exciting six — パリーグ

Pacific League パシフィック・リーグ
07

Orix Buffaloes

Osaka / HyogoHome — Kyocera Dome OsakaEst. 1936 / 2005 merger

Formed from a 2005 merger, the Buffaloes play in two cities and carry the fierce spirit of Kansai baseball into the modern era.

08

Fukuoka SoftBank Hawks

FukuokaHome — Mizuho PayPay DomeEst. 1938

The reigning Japan Series champions (2025, Hotaka Yamakawa MVP) and three straight Pacific League pennant winners, 2024–26. Fast, aggressive baseball and a booming mascot duo.

09

Saitama Seibu Lions

SaitamaHome — Belluna DomeEst. 1949

The "blue machine" — a historic powerhouse of the 1980s and 90s whose golden era still defines their identity today.

10

Hokkaido Nippon-Ham Fighters

SapporoHome — ES CON Field HokkaidoEst. 1946

Baseball at the northern frontier — the Fighters bring big-league energy to Japan's snowy north, in a stunning new ballpark.

11

Chiba Lotte Marines

ChibaHome — Zozo Marine StadiumEst. 1950

The cheerful club on Tokyo Bay, famous for colorful mascots, dance routines and one of the liveliest game-day atmospheres in Japan.

12

Tohoku Rakuten Golden Eagles

SendaiHome — Rakuten Mobile Saikyo Park MiyagiEst. 2005

NPB's newest franchise and a symbol of Tohoku's recovery — already a Japan Series champion in 2013.

CS

Road to the title

How the season ends

01

Regular season

Each team plays 143 games from spring to autumn. The top three clubs in each league advance to the playoffs.

02

Climax Series

NPB's unique playoff: the 2nd- and 3rd-place teams battle first, then face the league leader — who starts with a one-win advantage.

03

Japan Series

The Central and Pacific champions meet in a best-of-seven finale — in 2026, the SMBC Japan Series opens at Koshien on October 24 and runs to November 1.

Did you know? The Pacific League swings the bat for the pitcher — the Central League doesn't. From late May to mid-June, every team plays 18 interleague games — three against each of the six clubs in the other league.
