1. Information we collect

NPB Guide is a static, read-first website. We do not require accounts, and we do not collect personal data from visitors. Our contact and newsletter forms are demonstrations: the text you enter is processed only in your browser and is not sent to or stored on any server.

2. Cookies and advertising

This site participates in the Google AdSense advertising program. Google uses cookies — including the DART cookie — to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. This is done by Google, not by us, and is how the site earns the revenue that keeps it free to read.

  • You can opt out of personalized (interest-based) ads at adssettings.google.com.
  • You can manage cookies in your browser settings; disabling cookies may reduce ad relevance.
  • We do not currently run our own analytics tracker on this site.
No data sold. No data harvested. Just ads.

3. Third parties

Our pages load web fonts from Google Fonts and may embed content from YouTube or official league sources. Those third parties operate under their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to read.

4. Children's privacy

NPB Guide is a general-interest sports publication. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site grows. The "last updated" date at the top will change, and material changes will be noted on the contact page announcement area.

6. Contact us about privacy

Questions about your data or this policy: hello@npb-guide.example. See also our editorial policy and contact page.
