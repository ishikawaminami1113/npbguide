2026 DeNA vs SoftBank — the rematch

The matchup: DeNA vs SoftBank

The 2026 SMBC Japan Series is Yokohama DeNA BayStars vs. Fukuoka SoftBank Hawks — a rematch of 2024, when DeNA won 4–2 after trailing 2–0. SoftBank comes in as the three-time defending Central… no, Pacific League champion. DeNA earned their fifth Japan Series berth by sweeping the Hanshin Tigers in the Climax Series Final Stage, 4–1 counting the advantage. SoftBank reached their third straight Japan Series by sweeping Nippon-Ham 3–0 in the Pacific final, counting their two-game advantage.

Tickets

The Lawson advance lottery (October 14–17) has closed. General sale opens October 22 at noon JST. Series tickets sell fast — book early if you plan to attend.

How to watch from abroad

NPB games are broadcast on Japanese national TV and streamed through official NPB platforms. If you're outside Japan, read our watching guide for legal streaming options — and remember the time zone: an 18:30 first pitch in Yokohama is early morning in the Americas.

The storylines

  • 2024 rematch: DeNA overturned a 2–0 deficit to beat SoftBank 4–2 in 2024. Can SoftBank get revenge?
  • SoftBank's three-peat: The Hawks are chasing their third straight Japan Series title and 13th overall.
  • DeNA's underdog run: Finishing third in the regular season, then winning the Climax Series from the First Stage — DeNA is the first team to reach the Japan Series from that path.
