Lotte
Marines Chiba's seagulls

Teams Updated October 2026 5 min read By NPB Guide Editorial

The Marines own two of the strangest chapters in Japan Series history: the most one-sided sweep ever (33–4) and the first title by a third-place team.

The club

The Chiba Lotte Marines play at ZOZO Marine Stadium in Mihama Ward, Chiba City — their home since 1992, with room for about 30,000 fans. A founding member of the Pacific League in 1950 (as the Mainichi Orions), the team wears black, grey and white, and its mascots are Mar-kun, Rine-chan and Zu-chan.

Four titles

The Marines have won the Japan Series four times: 1950 (the inaugural series, as the Mainichi Orions), 1974 (as the Lotte Orions), 2005 and 2010.

The 33–4 sweep

In the 2005 Japan Series, the Marines swept the Hanshin Tigers in four games, outscoring them 33–4 — the most one-sided series in history. Manager Bobby Valentine became the first foreign manager to win the Japan Series, and Toshiaki Imae was named MVP.

2010: the third-place miracle

In 2010 the Marines finished third in the Pacific League — then became the first third-place team ever to win the Japan Series, beating the Chunichi Dragons 4–2–1 in a seven-game epic that included a 15-inning tie.

33 runs, 4 runs, one sweep — the most lopsided final in Japan Series history.
