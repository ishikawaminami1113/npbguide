Stadium
food A ballpark food tour

Food May 2026 5 min read By NPB Guide Editorial

Japanese ballparks are food festivals with a baseball game happening nearby. Here's our ranked tour of the best stadium eats — with prices, so you can plan your budget.

The essentials

  • Takoyaki (¥500–) — crispy octopus balls; the classic ballpark snack, sold nearly everywhere.
  • Yakitori (¥400–) — grilled chicken skewers with tare sauce, perfect between innings.
  • Beer (¥700–) — ordered from roaming vendors (uriko) who dash through the aisles.
  • Soft ice cream (¥400–) — the unofficial dessert of Japanese baseball.

The park specialties

Each stadium has its own signature. Stadium curry (¥800–) is famous at several parks — some are almost as famous as the teams. Winter night games call for ramen (¥900–), and Koshien's confectionery stalls are a tradition in themselves.

Eat the ballpark. Remember the game.

Budget tips

Bring cash — many stands don't take cards. Prices are gentle by stadium standards worldwide: ¥2,000–3,000 (about $15–20) covers a meal and a beer for one person. For etiquette around ordering from the uriko vendors, see the Game-Day Guide.

The verdict

Food is not a side dish at NPB — it's half the experience. Come hungry, queue confidently, and try whatever the locals are eating.
