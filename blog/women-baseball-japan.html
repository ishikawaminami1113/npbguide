Women's
baseball The rising story

Culture September 2026 5 min read By NPB Guide Editorial

Japan is one of the strongest homes of women's baseball in the world — with a national team that consistently contends for global titles and a grassroots pipeline that keeps growing.

The national team

The Japanese women's national team is a powerhouse at the Women's Baseball World Cup, winning multiple world titles and setting the standard for the sport. Games draw growing audiences, and the team's success has made international headlines in recent years.

The domestic scene

Women's baseball in Japan runs on corporate and club leagues — teams sponsored by companies and regions, a structure with deep roots in Japanese sports. The level is high, the dedication is professional, and the community is tight-knit: players often balance jobs and training year-round.

The future of Japanese baseball is played by everyone.

Why it matters

NPB's story is incomplete without the women's game: the same culture — the cheering, the discipline, the community — exists across all of Japanese baseball. For international fans, following the women's national team is a great way to discover a parallel world of the sport.

How to follow

Watch for World Cup coverage and domestic league results through official channels and highlights. If you're in Japan during a tournament, tickets are affordable and the atmosphere is welcoming.
