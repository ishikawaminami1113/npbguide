2026 Japan Series The complete postseason guide

Practical October 9, 2026 6 min read By NPB Guide Editorial

The regular season is over. Here is everything that happens next — from the First Stage on October 10 to a possible Game 7 on November 1. Updated October 16, 2026.

Where things stand

Both finalists are decided. The 2026 Japan Series is DeNA vs. SoftBank — the same meeting as 2024.

  • Central — Final Stage: DeNA won the First Stage over the Giants, then swept the Hanshin Tigers in four games — 2–0, 1–0, 4–0 and an 8–5 comeback — to reach the Japan Series for the fifth time, winning the stage 4–1 counting Hanshin's advantage.
  • Pacific — Final Stage: SoftBank dismissed Nippon-Ham in three games (6–4, 6–2, 6–2), clinching a third straight Japan Series berth.
  • Japan Series: opens October 24 at Yokohama Stadium, DeNA's home.

Status as of October 18, 2026 (JST). Scores and records on this page are updated as series progress; always confirm on the official NPB site before travelling.

The pennant race is over

Central League: the Hanshin Tigers clinched on October 3 — Hanshin's eighth pennant and the first back-to-back league title since the two-league system began in 1950 (79–62–2). Yomiuri (76–64–3) and DeNA (71–69–3) join them in the playoff.

Pacific League: the SoftBank Hawks clinched on September 17 — a third straight pennant (91–49–3) — on the league's best run production (707 runs, .261 batting average). Seibu (79–60–4) and Nippon-Ham (78–62–3) complete the playoff field.

The Climax Series (Oct 10–20)

  • First stage, Oct 10–12 (best-of-three): Giants vs. DeNA at Tokyo Dome; Seibu vs. Nippon-Ham at Belluna Dome. Result: DeNA and Nippon-Ham advanced.
  • Final stage, Oct 14 onward: the winners travel to the pennant winners — Hanshin at Koshien and SoftBank at the Mizuho PayPay Dome. Both stages began October 14.

A new rule in 2026

For the first time, the pennant winner can be handed a two-game advantage and the stage extended to as many as seven games. The rule triggers when the first-stage winner finished 10 or more games behind the pennant winner, or when that challenger finished below .500.

In 2026 the trigger applies in the Pacific League but not the Central. SoftBank finished 11.5 games ahead of Seibu and 13 ahead of Nippon-Ham, so whichever challenger advances must win five games while SoftBank needs three. In the Central, Hanshin's margins — 2.5 over the Giants, 7.5 over DeNA — keep the conventional format: six games, first to four.

The Japan Series (Oct 24 – Nov 1)

The SMBC Japan Series 2026 opens on Saturday, October 24 at Yokohama Stadium, DeNA's home (first game 18:30). Games 1–2 are in Yokohama, Games 3–5 move to the Mizuho PayPay Dome Fukuoka for SoftBank, and Games 6–7 return to Yokohama if needed — a possible Game 7 on Sunday, November 1.

Tickets: the Lawson first lottery (October 13–15) and the advance lottery (October 14–17, 18:00) have now closed. General sale opens October 22 at noon — series tickets move fast.

The storylines

  • A 2024 rematch: DeNA beat SoftBank 4–2 in the 2024 Japan Series after trailing 2–0. The same two clubs meet again, and DeNA is chasing a fourth title since 2024.
  • What happened to Hanshin: the Tigers became the first club to win back-to-back Central League titles since 1950 — then were swept 4–1, losing starters Teruaki Sato and Shota Morishita to injury.
  • The Hawks' three-peat: can the dynasty add a 13th Japan Series title to three straight pennants?
October in Japan: where every game feels like Game 7.

How to watch

In Japan, games air on national TV and official NPB streaming. From abroad, see our watch guide for legal options — and remember the time zone: an evening game in Japan is a morning game in New York.
