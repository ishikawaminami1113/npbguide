Nippon-Ham
Fighters Baseball in the snow

Teams Updated October 2026 5 min read By NPB Guide Editorial

The Hokkaido Nippon-Ham Fighters prove that great baseball doesn't need a warm climate. Based in Hokkaido since 2004 — and at ES CON Field Hokkaido in Kitahiroshima since 2023 — the club has turned Japan's northern frontier into one of the sport's most distinctive homes.

The move north

Founded in 1946 and long based in Tokyo, the Fighters relocated to Hokkaido in 2004 — a bold experiment in expanding NPB's map. The gamble paid off: Hokkaido embraced the team, and the club won the 2006 and 2016 Japan Series titles in its new home.

ES CON Field Hokkaido

In 2023 the Fighters moved into ES CON Field Hokkaido, a stadium built from the ground up with naming rights and fan experience in mind — a rare, striking ballpark in Kitahiroshima with a heated design suited to the climate. It's one of the most modern venues in the league.

Winter in Hokkaido is brutal. The baseball is not.

The identity

The Fighters' brand mixes the energy of a big-city club with the intimacy of a regional one. Their mascots — Byron and FIGHTY — make night games feel like festivals, and the club's navy-and-gold colors are common winter wear across the island.

Why it matters

The Fighters' story is really about NPB's geography: baseball thrives far beyond Tokyo and Osaka. For a visitor, a game at ES CON Field Hokkaido — with hot stadium ramen — is an unforgettable contrast to the summer heat of Koshien.

2026: back in October

The Fighters finished third (78–62–3) and return to the Climax Series — the First Stage opens at Belluna Dome on October 10 against the Saitama Seibu Lions. A hot October in snow country is exactly how this club writes its best stories.
