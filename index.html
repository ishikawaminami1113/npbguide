Why NPB?

Three good reasons

A /

The crowd

Ōendan cheer squads, drums, brass bands and 40,000 voices singing the same fight song. It's less a game, more a festival.

B /

The food

Takoyaki, yakitori, stadium curry. Japanese ballparks are food festivals with a baseball game happening nearby.

C /

The game

No clock, ties allowed, designated hitter only in the Pacific League. A distinctive, cerebral brand of baseball.
